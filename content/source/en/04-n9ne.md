## 4.1 NOBLED Specialty Coffee

**CARD**
Etiquetas · Branding · 2023
Meta *(tuyo)* · N9NE · Berlin, 2023
Título · NOBLED Specialty Coffee
Descripción *(tuyo)* · Complete identity for a specialty coffee brand built around the stories of Colombian coffee growers.
Tags *(tuyo)* · Identity · Brandbook
Filtros · Diseño gráfico · Estrategia

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · NOBLED Specialty Coffee

**Descripción**
A specialty coffee brand in Berlin, built around the stories of Colombian coffee growers. My first official branding.

**Overview**
Cliente · N9NE
Mi rol · Lead designer and strategist
Tipo de proyecto · Identity, experience and business model

**01 Challenge**
NOBLED was born from a visit to the farm of Silvio, a Colombian coffee grower and exporter. Specialty coffee is a saturated category in Berlin, and to complicate things, the coffee didn't arrive in time for the harvest.

**02 Approach**
We used that time to research the market. Specialty drinkers in Berlin buy in cafés, and what holds them back at home is the mess and the repetition of preparing a second cup. I designed the identity and the brandbook, the buying experience and a subscription model, with a format built around that finding: fresh coffee drip bags in boxes of eight, for one week.

**03 Result**
More than €400 in subscriptions in under a month, and an identity applied from the packaging to the point of sale.

## 4.2 Eat's Real

**CARD**
Etiquetas · Branding · 2023
Meta *(tuyo)* · N9NE · Seoul, 2023
Título · Eat's Real
Descripción *(tuyo)* · Brand and pieces for a healthy lunch delivery aimed at international students.
Tags *(tuyo)* · Branding · Packaging
Filtros · Diseño gráfico · UX/UI

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · Eat's Real

**Descripción**
A healthy lunch delivery for international students in Seoul, designed so you can order without speaking Korean and without a Korean bank account.

**Propósito** · Eat smart · Stay simple · No Korean bank account

**Overview**
Cliente · N9NE
Mi rol · Lead designer and UX
Tipo de proyecto · Brand, web and ordering experience

**01 Challenge**
International students in Seoul couldn't easily order delivery, and what they managed to order was usually unhealthy food. Language, payment methods and the lack of a Korean bank account piled up on every order. We were foreigners in Seoul, and we lived the problem ourselves.

**02 Approach**
I designed the experience for both sides: the student, who picks a weekly plan and orders without friction, and the restaurant, which needs to receive orders from someone who doesn't speak its language. I built the identity, the palette, the marketing pieces and the flyers with QR codes for campuses, and the website with the development team.

**03 Result**
It reached the point of operating with registered users, partner restaurants and real orders. A small pilot, working outside the paper.

## 4.3 Hola Guesthouse

**CARD**
Etiquetas · Branding · 2023
Meta *(tuyo)* · N9NE · Jeju, 2023
Título · Hola Guesthouse
Descripción *(tuyo)* · Brand system for a guesthouse designed as a home and not a hotel, for international travelers.
Tags *(tuyo)* · Identity · Guidelines
Filtros · Diseño gráfico

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · Hola Guesthouse

**Descripción**
A guesthouse on Jeju island designed as a home for international travelers, with a brand built around hospitality.

**Overview**
Cliente · Hola Guesthouse
Mi rol · Branding
Tipo de proyecto · Brand strategy and identity

**01 Challenge**
Most lodging in Jeju is designed for Korean travelers. Hola Guesthouse wanted someone from abroad to arrive on the island and feel at home from the first contact.

**02 Approach**
I defined the positioning, the audience and the key messages, and designed the visual identity with logo, palette and typography. Everything ended up in brand guidelines, so that every touchpoint, from search to arrival, looked and felt the same.

**03 Result**
A complete brand system, designed to feel like a home and ready to be read in Korean and in English.
