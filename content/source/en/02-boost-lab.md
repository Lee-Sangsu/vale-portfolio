## 2.1 Global Youth: Women's Entrepreneurship Summit

**CARD**
Etiquetas · Summit · Bogotá
Meta · BOOST LAB · Bogotá, 2024
Título · Global Youth: Women's Entrepreneurship Summit
Descripción *(tuyo)* · BOOST LAB's biggest event, built from scratch with a network of 45+ partners.
Tags · Event · Partnerships
Filtros · Eventos y talleres · Marketing y contenido · Estrategia

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · Global Youth: Women's Entrepreneurship Summit

**Descripción**
A free summit in Bogotá on September 21, 2024, for young women and students who want to start a business. We arrived in Colombia without contacts and left with more than 45 partner institutions and 400 people in the room.

**Propósito** · Capital · Mentoring · Network

**Overview**
Cliente · BOOST LAB
Mi rol · Concept, partnerships, speakers, marketing, design and operations
Tipo de proyecto · In-person summit

**01 Challenge**
Young women in Colombia face three barriers when they start a business: capital, mentoring and network. BOOST LAB was born in Seoul and had no network in Bogotá. We had to build one from scratch and, with it, a free event for hundreds of people, with a team of two.

**02 Approach**
We built the network through LinkedIn and by going out into the streets. I reached out to about 55 organizations, and to each one we presented the specific problem it could help solve from where it stood. We picked the two topics that were moving Colombia at that moment, women and internationalization, and partnered with universities to fill the room. My partner took care of technology, web, finance and legal.

**03 Result**
400+ attendees, 40 entrepreneur stands, more than 45 partner institutions and 18 speakers, including Senator Andrea Padilla, Joanna Prieto from Geek Girls LATAM and Paola Andrea Franco from Fondo Impacta. The campaign reached 142,473 views and attendees rated the summit 4.8 out of 5.

**Partners**

**Design**

## 2.2 Sejong Global Idea Hackathon

**CARD**
Etiquetas · Hackathon · Seoul
Meta · BOOST LAB · Seoul, 2025
Título · Sejong Global Idea Hackathon
Descripción *(tuyo)* · Two days to turn an idea into a pitch in front of a jury.
Tags · Hackathon · Program design
Filtros · Eventos y talleres · Diseño gráfico · Estrategia

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · Sejong Global Idea Hackathon

**Descripción**
Two days at Sejong University to take an idea from zero to a pitch in front of a jury, with 19 entrepreneurs from 14 countries. BOOST LAB × Bnomad × Sejong University Campus Town, December 12 and 13, 2025.

**Propósito** · Learning by doing · Learning by reflecting · Diverse teams

**Overview**
Cliente · Sejong University Campus Town, with Bnomad
Mi rol · Graphic design, marketing, modules, templates and activities
Tipo de proyecto · Hackathon and training program

**01 Challenge**
Almost every hackathon ends with a pitch and a photo. We wanted people to leave with transferable skills, in nine effective hours spread over two days, with participants who mostly arrived alone.

**02 Approach**
We designed a program with pre-assigned teams and drawn problems, so diversity lived inside each team and nobody started with an advantage. I designed the workshops and the nine working templates, from the Me-Us-Customer to the nine-slide investment deck, and led the pitch track. The program closed with a two-hour debrief instead of an awards ceremony.

**03 Result**
4.73 out of 5 in satisfaction, with 100% of participants answering the survey. Five out of five teams made it to the pitch in front of the jury, with no dropouts. And all five proposed, on their own, solutions that combine AI with human support: the first prize was called More Human.

**Secciones extra**

**Participants**
19 entrepreneurs from Spain, the United States, Russia, Kenya, Indonesia, France, Finland, Uzbekistan, Israel, Canada, India, Kyrgyzstan, Korea, Andorra and Colombia, in five teams.

**The projects**
*Homeaway Hub · More Human.* Health system navigation with AI and verified human companions.
*Snail Agency · Most Cultural.* Korean workplace culture coaching for foreigners.
*Hospital Gaja · Most Solid.* Multilingual plugin for medical documents.
*Find My House.* AI plus expert advice for rental contracts.
*Solvisa.* Visa paperwork with AI and advisors.

**The templates**
Nine working worksheets: team identity, the foreigner's situation, problem definition, ideation, nine-slide deck, pitch tips and debrief, among others. All the material was produced in less than a month.

**Outreach**
759 visits to the registration page on the peak day, four days before the event. Half of the traffic came from referrals and organic, and 77% from Seoul.

**Research**
The hackathon was the pilot for my thesis, "Designing an AI-enabled Hybrid Incubation Program for Young International Entrepreneurs", graded 8.3.

Etiquetas · International expansion
Meta · BOOST LAB · Seoul, Busan and Jeju, 2025
Título · International Missions
Descripción *(tuyo)* · Two Latin American companies and two sector routes inside the Korean ecosystem.
Tags · International expansion · Experiences
Filtros · Eventos y talleres · Estrategia

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · International Missions

**Descripción**
Fifteen days in Seoul, Busan and Jeju with two Latin American companies, from July 25 to August 8, 2025. Every meeting, every transfer and every translation, designed around the company that travels.

**Overview**
Clientes · Caja Lab and Momento
Mi rol · Itinerary design, meeting schedule, translation and support
Tipo de proyecto · Business and cultural mission

**01 Challenge**
Entering Korea from Latin America runs into the language first, and then into something harder: reaching the right people. Two companies from opposite industries, innovation and specialty coffee, needed different routes within the same trip.

**02 Approach**
We curated an itinerary by industry and ran two routes in parallel, with the cultural experience shared. We scheduled six institutional meetings and translated live between Korean and Spanish, with English as a bridge depending on the sector. We accompanied them from start to finish, with context before each meeting, a debrief after, and printed itineraries and welcome materials.

**03 Result**
We beat the goal of five companies connected by industry, and deals came out of it, mostly on the coffee side. Collaboration conversations opened between Bogotá and Seoul, and between Medellín and Busan. Caja Lab now organizes its own international missions.

**Secciones extra**

**Innovation route · Caja Lab**
Impact Square · HBM · Underdogs · MYSC · Antler · Localo · Bnomad

**Coffee route · Momento**
Soft Pack · MANO Master · LOWKEY · Coffee Buy · Werk Foundation · CUPING · Baske Coffee Shop

**The cultural layer**
Hanbok at Gyeongbokgung, markets, a cruise on the Han River, Spa Land in Busan, a haenyeo show and sunrise at Seongsan. Entering a new market starts with reading its culture first.

**The network that remained**
MoU signed with Konkuk University, and relationships with Sungkyunkwan, Gyeongsang National University, Seoul Women's University and Jeju National University.

## 2.5 Seoul Entrepreneur Summit

**CARD**
Etiquetas · Summit · Seoul

Descripción · BOOST LAB's first event: Korean and international entrepreneurs in the same room.
Tags · Event · Identity
Filtros · Eventos y talleres · Diseño gráfico

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · Seoul Entrepreneur Summit

**Descripción**
The event BOOST LAB was born with, in March 2024: an afternoon of expert talks, a team challenge and a pitch, with Korean and international entrepreneurs mixed at every table.

**Overview**
Cliente · BOOST LAB with N9NE
Mi rol · Concept, visual identity, campaign and operations
Tipo de proyecto · In-person summit

**01 Challenge**
In Seoul there were events for Korean entrepreneurs and events for internationals, almost never for both at once. We wanted a real challenge to work on in mixed teams, and getting a company to propose one took months.

**02 Approach**
We built the cases with former McKinsey consultants, so the challenge depended on us and on no sponsor. We designed five timed hours: a talk with questions, ice breaking, a team challenge with research and validation, and a pitch. Twenty tables in four quadrants by sector; each quadrant voted for its winner without being able to vote for itself, and the winners presented in front of a jury.

**03 Result**
80 attendees at BOOST LAB's first event, and the base format for everything we did afterwards. Three months later came the Seoul Women Entrepreneur Summit, and six months after that, Bogotá.

**Secciones extra**

**The agenda**
Welcome · expert talk with live questions · ice breaking · team challenge · pitch by quadrant · final pitch in front of a jury · networking

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · Ruta 8 · Casa Colombia

**Descripción**
A program by the Consulate General of Colombia in Quito for Colombian entrepreneurs in Ecuador, within the entrepreneurship and employability line of COLOMBIA NOS UNE. We designed the whole route, from the open call to the eight workshops, between August and December 2026.

**Propósito** · Call · Train · Connect

**Overview**
Cliente · Consulate General of Colombia in Quito · COLOMBIA NOS UNE
Mi rol · Program design, open call campaign, pieces, content and participant support
Tipo de proyecto · Training program and campaign

**01 Challenge**
The Colombian entrepreneurial community in Ecuador was scattered, and the Consulate needed to reach it, explain a new program and turn interest into real applications. There was one delicate point: registering did not mean being selected, and that had to be clear from the first message. All of it under the Casa Colombia identity.

**02 Approach**
We designed the route in three stages: the open call, eight workshops split between in-person and virtual, and a closing. For the open call we built four entrepreneur profiles, each with its own message, and a system of pieces on top of the Casa Colombia identity: two carousels a week throughout the whole cycle, seven emails, two live information sessions and a registration page that records which channel brings in each person.

**03 Result**
176 applications and 46 businesses selected for the cohort, which are now moving through the eight workshops. Participants also have their own platform where they follow the program session by session, with direct access from their email. The campaign sustained two pieces a week throughout the whole cycle, from the open call to the last workshop.

**Descripción**
An intimate workshop in Seoul, in April 2024, to talk as a community about something that is almost never said out loud: how to hold yourself up while you build something.

**Overview**

## 2.9 BOOST LAB Workshops

**CARD**
Etiquetas · Workshops · 2024 - 2026
Meta · BOOST LAB · Colombia and Korea, 2024 - 2026
Título · BOOST LAB Workshops
Descripción · 45 minutes to work on your own brand live and leave with the templates filled in.
Tags · Workshops · Templates
Filtros · Eventos y talleres · Estrategia

**PÁGINA**

**Título** · BOOST LAB Workshops

**Descripción**
BOOST LAB's training line: short, practical workshops on brand, strategy and entrepreneurship. There is only one rule. Each person arrives with their project, works on it during the workshop and leaves with their own strategy written on paper.

**Propósito** · Work live · See the method from the inside · Leave with something written

**Overview**
Cliente · BOOST LAB, for entrepreneurs, small brands, students and company teams
Mi rol · Content design, templates and facilitation
Tipo de proyecto · Line of in-person and custom workshops

**01 Challenge**
People who start a business leave many talks inspired, and a week later they still have the same questions about their brand. We wanted a short format that fit into an afternoon, an event or a company's calendar, and that left each person with decisions made about their own project.

**02 Approach**
We designed a format of about 45 minutes that alternates four moments: a short block of content, a template to solve on the spot, a "behind the scenes" where we show how we do it in our own projects, and an open space for questions. Each workshop comes with its templates on A4, designed to be filled in by hand and taken home.

**03 Result**
Three named workshops, one-to-one versions for a single brand and a custom wellbeing workshop, which a company asked us to run for its team after the Seoul Wellness Workshop. The same method sustains the workshops of the Sejong Hackathon, Global Youth and Ruta 8.

**Secciones extra**

**What a workshop looks like**
01 Short content, straight to the point
02 Live template on each person's project
03 Behind the scenes: how we do it ourselves
04 Open questions

**The lineup**
*Brands that grow · Identity, social media and strategy.* Four blocks, clarity, social media, judgment and strategy, with one A4 template per block.
*Brand with Purpose · Strategy, visibility and digital future.*
*Entrepreneurship with Meaning · Wellbeing, impact and global vision.*
*One to one.* The same method applied to a single personal brand, in six sessions.
*Custom.* Workshops for company teams, like the wellbeing one that grew out of the Seoul Wellness Workshop.

**What each person takes away**
Their filled-in templates · decisions made about their brand · a method to repeat it on their own
